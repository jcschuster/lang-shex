# codemirror-lang-shex

[ShEx](https://shex.io) language support for [CodeMirror 6](https://codemirror.net/): a
Lezer parser for the compact syntax ShExC, generated from the grammar of the Shape
Expressions Language 2.1, Final Community Group Report, 8 October 2019 (recorded in
`package.json` under `shex.specification`), with highlighting, folding and indentation of
shapes, groups and value sets, and completion of keywords.

```js
import {EditorView, basicSetup} from "codemirror"
import {shex} from "codemirror-lang-shex"

new EditorView({extensions: [basicSetup, shex()], parent: document.body})
```

`shex()` returns the `LanguageSupport`; `shexLanguage` is the `LRLanguage`.

Nodes are named after the productions of the specification in CamelCase (`ShapeExprDecl`,
`TripleConstraint`, `ValueSet`, `ShapeRef`, ...) and its terminals likewise (`Iriref`,
`PnameLn`, `Langtag`, `StringLiteral2`); productions that only pass one child through, and
the levels of `OR`, `AND` and `NOT`, are no nodes. Keywords are tokens named by their text
(`PREFIX`, `start`, `CLOSED`). Comments are `LineComment` and `BlockComment`, string
literals sit in `String`, brackets are the tokens `"("`, `")"`, `"{"`, `"}"`, `"["`, `"]"`,
marked with their partners. The punctuation `.`, `|`, `~`, `-`, `=`, `^^`, `$`, `&`, `%`, `;`,
`,` and `//` are tokens named by their text; the `%` that ends a semantic action without code
sits in `EmptyCode`.

What the tests show: every production and terminal of the specification's grammar, as
amended by the deviations below, is reachable from the top rule of the generated grammar
(whitespace and comments from its skip rule), except `shapeAtomNoRef`, which no production
uses; every token matches what its terminal
matches on samples generated from the terminal and edits of them; the test cases cover
every node type; of the ShEx test suite
(shexSpec/shexTest at `ea33621c`), every schema parses without error nodes (515 files,
including 24 that no manifest lists), and every negative syntax test gets an error node
except 10 whose errors are not syntax errors (label collisions, repeated facets, a numeric
facet on an unknown datatype, undeclared prefixes). The negative structure tests parse, as
their errors are not syntactic; two of them, an inclusion in parentheses with a cardinality
or a label, are rejected by shex.js but derived by the grammar.

Deviations from the published grammar, each listed in `spec/README.md` of the repository:

- the three fixes made upstream after publication: several exclusions after one wildcard
  (`[. - <a> - <b>]`), all of one kind as in shex.js (`[. - <a> - 'x']` is an error), IRI
  exclusions after an IRI stem, no `.` at the end of a prefixed name;
- `$-\[` in `REGEXP` read as three characters, not as a range;
- the ShEx 2.next syntax as shex.js parses it: `ABSTRACT`, `EXTENDS` or `&`, `RESTRICTS` or
  `-`; annotations and semantic actions on node constraints; `,` between triple expressions;
- keywords in any case, except `true` and `false`, as in shex.js, which however does not
  read a lower-case `not` as `NOT`.

## Generated and hand-written files

In the [lang-shex](https://github.com/jcschuster/lang-shex) repository:

- Extracted by `gen/fetch.mjs`: `spec/shex-2.1.ebnf`, from the specification's page.
- Generated by `gen/ebnf2lezer.mjs` (`pnpm generate`): `src/shex.grammar` from
  `spec/shex-2.1.ebnf`, `spec/deviations.ebnf` and `gen/overlay.grammar`, and `src/words.ts`
  (the keywords) from the productions.
- Hand-written: `spec/deviations.ebnf`, `gen/overlay.grammar` (the top rule, the hidden and
  unused productions, the case of keywords, one precedence, the skip rule, the named bracket
  tokens; each explained in `gen/CONFLICTS.md`), `src/highlight.ts`, `src/index.ts` and
  `src/shex.grammar.d.ts`.
